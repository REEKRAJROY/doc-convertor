    Why this is private

    All processing runs in your browser using Canvas and WebAssembly. There is no upload endpoint and no server-side storage — open DevTools → Network and watch: your files generate zero requests.

    Files are processed on your device and never transmitted. Third-party ad and CDN scripts may load and can see your IP address, but never your file contents.
